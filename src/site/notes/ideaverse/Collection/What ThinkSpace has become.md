I have a digital workspace for my research, thinking and writing, which goes by the name of [ThinkSpace](https://anapoly.co.uk/labs/thinkspace/). Since adding an AI assistant to it, it has become an essential part of the way I do things.

A recent piece of work made me reflect on what that means, and the result is a short case study: [Liaison, Leverage and Learning](https://anapoly.netlify.app/ideaverse/collection/liaison-leverage-and-learning/).

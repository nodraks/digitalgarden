# Goal-Directed Context Management

*Transparency label: AI-assisted*

Goal-Directed Context Management explains how to organise collaboration with AI so its contributions stay relevant, precise and grounded through the development of a knowledge-based product. Contextual scaffolding gives the AI the right background, an up-to-date picture of progress and clear direction for the next step, in the way a satnav updates in real time.

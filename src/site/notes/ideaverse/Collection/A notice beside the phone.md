# A notice beside the phone

*Written by Alec Fearon on 5 September 2026 in Anapoly Diary*
*Transparency label: Human-only*
*<--- [[ideaverse/Collection/What ThinkSpace has become\|What ThinkSpace has become]]*
*---> next entry*

Scammers are a growing menace, and I know someone who was nearly tricked into losing £25,000. When I chanced across a YouTube on this subject by Frank Delaney, a former fraud investigator, I was interested to see what he had to say. It turned out to be very good advice. 

This case study, [[ideaverse/Collection/How to deal with calls from scammers\|How to deal with calls from scammers]], explains how Peka helped me extract that advice and turn it into [a notice one could pin up beside the phone](https://www.anapoly.co.uk/files/five-rules-for-answering-an-unknown-call.pdf). It is the kind of work which happens quite often and, if carried out by a human alone, is tedious and time-consuming. When carried out with the assistance of Peka, it is almost effortless and takes very little of the human's time.

# The AI and Young People essay project

*Transparency label: AI-only*

On 7 August 2025 Anapoly ran a trial Acclimatisation Lab. One participant, a member of staff at Marjon University, suggested a collaboration between the university and Anapoly, and the conversation turned to the risk that students let AI think for them. Two pieces of writing began on 9 August 2025: the essay [[ideaverse/Collection/Shaping Human-AI Interaction\|Shaping Human-AI Interaction]], on how values, ethics and ethos can shape the relationship between people and AI, and [[ideaverse/Collection/A collaborative project\|A collaborative project]], the transcript of the chat with ChatGPT 5 that turned the conversation into a project.

The project was to write a concise essay, up to 1,500 words, on the dangers to young people, university students above all, of outsourcing their thinking to AI, and on whether responsible use of AI can reduce those dangers. It ran as a ChatGPT Project, and the notes below are its working papers: for the most part ChatGPT's own replies, kept as a record of how the project was set up. [[ideaverse/Collection/Rationale for the project\|Rationale for the project]] gives the case for the essay in two lengths, one for general readers and one for educators and policymakers, and [[ideaverse/Collection/Product Description - Essay\|Product Description - Essay]] says what the essay is, who it is for and how it might be used. [[ideaverse/Collection/Project Instructions for AI & Young People Essay\|Project Instructions for AI & Young People Essay]] is the instruction set the Project ran under.

Three notes record how ChatGPT was set up for the work. [[ideaverse/Collection/PHAIN startup prompt\|PHAIN startup prompt]] is ChatGPT's improved version of the starting prompt, [[ideaverse/Collection/PHAIN bootstrap clarification\|PHAIN bootstrap clarification]] the questions it asked before beginning, and [[ideaverse/Collection/PHAIN structure for project instructions\|PHAIN structure for project instructions]] the structure it recommended for the instructions. [[ideaverse/Collection/The distinction between Project Instructions and Project Files\|The distinction between Project Instructions and Project Files]] is its account of the difference between the two: the instructions say how the work is done, the files supply the material it draws on.

The remaining two notes turn to the literature review the essay needs. [[ideaverse/Collection/refined key questions for the literature review\|refined key questions for the literature review]] restates the project's four key questions in a form a review can answer, and [[ideaverse/Collection/team approach to literature review\|team approach to literature review]] proposes how a team of five would divide the reading between those four questions.

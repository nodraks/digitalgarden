# No substitute for reading the paper

*Written by Alec Fearon on 24 June 2025 in Anapoly Diary*
*Transparency label: Human-only*
*<--- [[ideaverse/Collection/ChatGPT models - which to use when\|ChatGPT models - which to use when]]*
*---> [[ideaverse/Collection/How ChatGPT helped draft our first acclimatisation lab setup\|How ChatGPT helped draft our first acclimatisation lab setup]]*

> … what I can say is that a theme throughout this self-analysis is this: I find ChatGPT to be a really useful tool when I already have some idea of what I want to do and when I’m actually *engaged* with the issue. I find it much less reliable or useful for completely automating parts of the process. There’s no substitute for reading the paper.

Source: Sean Trott in his newsletter [How I use (and don’t use) ChatGPT](https://open.substack.com/pub/seantrott/p/how-i-use-and-dont-use-chatgpt?r=xee5y&utm_campaign=post&utm_medium=web&showWelcomeOnShare=false) on 24 June 2025

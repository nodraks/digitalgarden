Some thoughts about what it means to be an [[ideaverse/Collection/2025-11-06-101131 AI-embedded business\|AI-embedded business]]. Also, small language models are coming into favour for focused tasks, as they are more accurate and more efficient. The approach is to combine them with large language models into [[ideaverse/Collection/2025-11-16-154431 Hybrid AI systems\|hybrid AI systems]]. That seems to be the direction small businesses will be going and therefore something we need to keep abreast of.
